Doctors and Nurses in Australia by where they trained

The column bar chart depicts the yearly distribution of foreign-trained and domestically trained medical professionals from 2012 to 2021. Each year, two bars are displayed side by side, representing the respective numbers of foreign-trained and domestically trained professionals. Hovering over each column reveals an interactive pie chart showcasing the proportion of doctors or nurses based on their training background for that specific year, offering succinct insights into workforce composition trends over time.
