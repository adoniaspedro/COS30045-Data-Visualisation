Doctors and Nurses in Australia

The stacked bar chart depicts the total number of doctors and nurses from 2012 to 2021. Each bar illustrates the distribution between doctors and nurses, while hovering over a segment displays a donut chart. These donut charts provide a detailed breakdown of the proportions of domestically trained and foreign-trained professionals, offering insights into the composition of the healthcare workforce over the years.
