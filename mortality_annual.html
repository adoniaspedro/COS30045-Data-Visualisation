Mortality Rate

The line graph represents a clear visual of the mortality rates that have occured between 2015 to 2022. By hovering over the dots on the lines for each country, it will reveal the number of deaths and the year for that country. Its a characteristic feature that makes the graph into a highly engaging tool, allowing you to explore and understand the data in great depth. It’s a great attribute that not only enhances the graph’s functionality but also provides a more immersive and informative experience for users.
